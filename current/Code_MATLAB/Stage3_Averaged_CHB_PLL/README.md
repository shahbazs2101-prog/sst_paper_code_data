## What changes from the verified pilot
Three explicit phase-current states obey L di/dt = vgrid - vconverter - R i, with L=51.354 mH and R=0.53778 ohm. SRF PLL uses amplitude-normalized quadrature voltage, natural frequency 20 Hz and damping 0.707. During voltage below 0.1 pu, it holds nominal frequency and freezes its integrator. Grid current PI gains start at Kp=L*2*pi*100 and Ki=R*2*pi*100, with decoupling/feedforward. Integral current action freezes during voltage saturation; active-current reference observes the assumed reactive-priority current circle. Actual overcurrent is logged and protection trips at 1.25 times the rated current vector norm; actual current is not artificially clipped.

SST converter phase voltage is limited by the sum of the three actual cell voltages. Cells within a phase share converter input power equally in this average allocation; this is a declared sharing assumption rather than an executed per-cell PWM balancing law. Each phase therefore produces natural twice-line-frequency power ripple in its links. The grid is balanced with no neutral current. CHB common-mode voltage is removed from the effective phase plant; limits apply to bridge-stack voltages before neutral adjustment.

The conventional VSC is referred through an ideal 11 kV/415 V LFT. Its voltage envelope uses common-mode injection and pole limits of half the instantaneous LV bus voltage times the turns ratio. Its device/modulation family is different from CHB. Both interfaces use the same MV-referred RL branch and current rating for this explicit electrical comparator. The LFT is ideal: leakage is represented only by this common branch, no core loss or saturation is modeled.

DAB power has a 0.2 ms first-order actuator and voltage-dependent instantaneous SPS feasibility. LV PI and proportional differential balancing retain their pilot starting gains. Forward power only, phase ratio 0–0.5; nonlinear commutation is excluded. Current-dependent filter losses are included, but semiconductor and magnetic losses are not. Initial currents include filter losses and total input/output power balance. Per-cell initial energies are nominal, not initialized to their eventual periodic 100 Hz ripple orbit. That startup ripple must be inspected rather than claimed as a steady-state initialized full switching model.

Integration is RK4 at the declared sample interval, with events aligned to the sample grid and endpoint evaluation just before the next event edge. Protection is latched from evaluated RK stages. Grid isolation after trip assumes inductor freewheeling through the filter resistance and zero delivered load, maintaining an energy ledger including inductor stored energy and resistive dissipation. This is an ideal protection abstraction, not an implemented breaker/snubber. Phase angles are not reset at events.

The matched conventional capacitor now matches TOTAL usable SST capacitor energy (MV links plus the 2 mF LV bus), approximately 238.0918 mF. This differs from the 236.0918 mF pilot arm, which matched MV energy alone. Do not combine their trip metrics as if configurations were identical. A 140 kW high-load test is used because a 150 kVA input interface with positive filter losses cannot deliver 150 kW steadily at unity power factor. Prior 150 kW lossless pilot results remain separate. No controller has been fitted to those prior results.

## Checks and limits
Energy ledger: change in all capacitor plus inductor energies equals integrated external grid input minus load delivery and filter loss. The discrete RK4 residual is checked against 0.05 J, not assumed to be exactly zero. Report convergence between both steps, current overshoot, modulation saturation and PLL error. The six scenarios include a persistent synthetic 15 degree phase jump with no voltage sag, alongside nominal, partial sag, short interruption, coincident net-demand step and high-load interruption. These are controlled synthetic tests; no NREL measurements are replayed.

This is an equation-based average circuit/control implementation in Simulink using a Level-2 MATLAB S-function. It is not a physically wired Simscape circuit or a switching model. No new MATLAB results are included in this source package. Author execution and review must precede manuscript insertion. If this stage fails a gate, retain and report the failure before changing gains or assumptions.
